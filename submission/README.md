## The picks against the market

A correct pick earns −log₂(p) points, where p is Kalshi's probability for that candidate. We back a candidate our forecast expects to lose only when:
1. backing them is worth at least 25% more on average than backing the favorite;
2. we rate them strictly higher than the market does;
3. we give them at least a 5% chance.

## Notes on the file
